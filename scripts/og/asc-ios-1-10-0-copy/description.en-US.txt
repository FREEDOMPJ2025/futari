Futari is a shared ledger for two people.

It isn't a budgeting app, and it doesn't grade your spending. The two of you note down everyday spending together, so who paid and how it splits is always clear. Look back a while later, and what you see is the life you've shared.

Record together, see together
・One ledger for both of you. Each of you adds entries, and you see the other's right away.
・Choose how each entry splits: half and half, by ratio, all mine or all theirs.
・See the balance at a glance. Who owes whom is worked out for you, and settling up is one step.
・Start on your own and invite your partner later.

Everyday money
・Record expenses and income. Set up recurring ones, like rent or subscriptions, once.
・When a recurring entry is due, the app lets you know, and it's added once you confirm it.
・Trips: give a trip its own ledger, record in local currencies and convert at the rates you set.
・Outings with friends: create an outing and share the link with the group. Friends open it in a browser and pick their name to add what they paid. No download or sign-up, up to 20 people. At the end, Futari shows who should pay whom, and the part between you two goes back into your main ledger.

iPhone Shortcuts
・Add the "Add Entry to Futari" action to a Wallet automation. After you pay with Apple Pay, the amount and merchant are filled in, and the entry opens for you to confirm.
・The same store gets the category you picked last time.
・You can also use a shortcut to send a purchase for you to confirm first. Only you can see it until you add it.

Look back together
・Monthly reviews: each month, last month's income and spending are gathered in one place, and you can leave a note for next month.
・Category breakdowns and chapter history show where the money went and how the days went by.

More than money
・Aibutsu, the things you care for: home, car, kids, pets, plants. Keep them in the same ledger, with related expenses filed under each one automatically.
・Log fuel for your car.
・Insurance: protection and savings policies on separate tabs, with the insured, beneficiaries and renewal dates on one page.

Moving from another app
・Import an export file from another budgeting app, or a CSV. Files are read on your device, and the original file isn't uploaded.

Peace of mind
・Only your two accounts can open the ledger.
・Connections are encrypted with HTTPS. Sensitive fields, such as a child's legal name or ID number, are encrypted again before they're stored.
・Delete your account anytime in Settings.

Free, with no ads. Works on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web, so you can share one ledger even on different phones.
Available in English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.

Two people, one ledger.
